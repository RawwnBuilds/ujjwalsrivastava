What is GEO and AEO content writing, and why does it matter more than traditional SEO right now?

+

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring content so that AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini pull from and cite it when answering user queries. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) focuses specifically on matching the question-and-answer format that AI engines use to retrieve information.

Traditional SEO targets the ten blue links. GEO and AEO target the answer box above those links, which is increasingly the only result a user reads. For B2B SaaS brands, this means structuring content with direct-answer opening paragraphs, clear entity definitions, and schema markup that signals topical authority to AI crawlers. Content built this way appears in Perplexity citations and Google AI Overviews, reaching buyers before they ever click a link.
