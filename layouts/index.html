Case: Editorial Operations

Building editorial infrastructure for multi-client SaaS publishing

Managing editorial for multiple B2B SaaS clients simultaneously meant the quality bar had to hold without manual oversight on every word. The solution was infrastructure: writer onboarding SOPs that compressed quality ramp time, client-specific style guides that traveled across writers, and a QA process that caught structural and factual errors before publication.

The result: new writers produce publishable work within their second week, revision loops shrink, and editorial standards hold at volume.

Case: GEO / AEO Framework

A content protocol for Perplexity answers and LLM citations

Most B2B content is structured for blue links. AI engines retrieve information differently: they prioritize entity clarity, direct answers, and topical density. The protocol starts with query modeling, asking what someone actually asks and how an AI system phrases the answer back.

Content is then structured to deliver that answer in the first paragraph, with supporting entities that establish authority. The result: client content appearing in AI Overview boxes and Perplexity citations for competitive SaaS keywords.

What is GEO and AEO content writing, and why does it matter more than traditional SEO right now?

+

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring content so that AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini pull from and cite it when answering user queries. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) focuses specifically on matching the question-and-answer format that AI engines use to retrieve information.

Traditional SEO targets the ten blue links. GEO and AEO target the answer that appears above them, which is increasingly the only result a user reads. For B2B SaaS brands, this means content built to appear in Perplexity citations and Google AI Overviews, reaching buyers before they ever click a link.
